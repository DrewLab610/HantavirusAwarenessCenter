What is Hantavirus?

Understanding the virus, its origins, and how it spreads.

Rodent-Borne

Rare viral disease spread mainly through infected rodents and their droppings.

Respiratory Impact

Can severely affect the lungs and breathing, leading to acute respiratory distress.

Severe Illness

May cause hantavirus pulmonary syndrome (HPS), a potentially fatal respiratory illness.

Limited Transmission

Human-to-human transmission is rare but possible in some hantavirus strains.

Safety & Prevention

Practical steps to reduce your risk of hantavirus exposure.

Wash Hands Frequently

Use soap and water or hand sanitiser regularly, especially before eating.

Avoid Rodent Contact

Do not touch or handle rodents. Report any sightings to authorities.

Wet-Clean Methods

Use wet mopping instead of dry sweeping to avoid aerosolising particles.

Wear Masks

Use face masks if respiratory symptoms appear or in high-risk areas.

Practice Distancing

Maintain physical distance during active outbreaks in your area.
